QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How do I apply for a loan?

Select your loan type, enter your requested amount, repayment period, income and loan purpose, then submit your application for review.

How long does approval take?

Applications are reviewed by an authorized administrator. The actual review time can vary.

Can I have more than one application?

The Globalcrest Capital system allows one pending loan application at a time. Once it has been reviewed, another application can be submitted if permitted.

Does approval automatically add money to my account?

No. Approval and loan disbursement are separate steps. An approved application must go through the appropriate disbursement process.
